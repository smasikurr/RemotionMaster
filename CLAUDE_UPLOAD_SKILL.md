## 🏛️ Unified Architecture & Philosophy

Instead of juggling fragmented repos and individual skills one-by-one, **Remotion Master** synthesizes every essential motion graphics discipline into one cohesive powerhouse:
1. **Remotion Core Engine** (`remotion-dev/remotion` & `remotion-claude-code-skill`): Frame-accurate 4K 60FPS rendering.
2. **221+ After Effects Primitives** (`lifeprompt-team/remotion-scenes`): Liquid, particle, HUD, kinetic text, and 3D themes.
3. **Calibrated Spring Physics** (`template-prompt-to-motion-graphics-saas`): Mathematical damped harmonic motion.
4. **Cinetic Motion Laws** (`Leonxlnx/cinetic`): 16th-beat timing grids and organic camera drift.
5. **Documentary & Explainer Direction** (`heygen/vox-explainer` & `heygen/session-story`): Collage pacing and story arcs.
6. **Design Intelligence & Copy** (`ui-ux-pro-max` & `marketingskills`): 97 palettes, 57 font pairings, and conversion copy.

## 🏎️ Calibrated Spring Physics Presets
- `snappy`: `{ damping: 20, stiffness: 200, mass: 1 }` (Snappy UI cards, buttons, badges)
- `bouncy`: `{ damping: 8, stiffness: 100, mass: 1 }` (Playful reveals, celebratory elements)
- `smooth`: `{ damping: 200, stiffness: 100, mass: 1 }` (Luxury typography, subtle camera drifts)
- `heavy`: `{ damping: 15, stiffness: 80, mass: 2 }` (Heavy objects, 3D hardware)
- `elastic`: `{ damping: 6, stiffness: 150, mass: 0.8 }` (Comic/cartoon bursts)

## 🎬 Prompt Writing Best Practices (Clear Prompts for Best Output)
When prompting the AI agent or Claude:
1. **Specify the Topic & Focal Point:** Define the hero element (e.g., "Hanging 50% Off Price Tag" or "Cyberpunk Tech Telemetry").
2. **Set Visual Tone & Colors:** Give exact hex codes or palettes (e.g., `#E3062A` deep red, `#00F0FF` cyan, `#00FF00` chroma green).
3. **Define Motion Pacing:** Mention entry physics (e.g., "elastic spring drop on frame 0-40"), loop period (e.g., "200-frame pendulum swing"), and exit behavior.
4. **Target Platform:** Specify 4K UHD (3840x2160) @ 60 FPS standard with pure chroma green for keying or pure black for Screen blend mode.
